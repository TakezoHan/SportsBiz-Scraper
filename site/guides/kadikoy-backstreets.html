Kadıköy backstreets: a six-stop mezze crawl in half a day.

One narrow block contains the entire argument for Istanbul food culture. Walk it slowly, in this order, with an appetite.

By Deniz Aksoy · 4 hours · ~₺1,200 pp including rakı

We walk this route about four times a year, and we always end the day on Moda Caddesi wondering why anyone bothers with the European side. Start at 11:30 — earlier if you're hungry, never later, or the Çiya queue eats the day.

1 · Şükrü Turşucusu (11:30)

The pickle primer. Beetroot juice in a small glass. You drink it standing. You do not eat anything here — you prime your palate and you move.

2 · Çiya Sofrası (11:45 – 12:45)

Straight to Çiya. Point at the steam table. Eat slowly but not slowly enough that the room turns over on you.

3 · Fazıl Bey (13:00)

Turkish coffee. One sugar. No phones. A fifteen-minute sit is mandatory.

4 · Borsam Taşfırın (13:30)

A single lahmacun each. Parsley, lemon, rolled. If you're full already, wrap one for later — do not skip.

5 · Ali Usta Dondurma (14:15)

Walk it down Moda Caddesi toward the sea. Sakızlı and kaymaklı.

6 · Viktor Levi Şarap Evi (15:15)

Garden seating. One shared meze tray. One fish. Two rakı. Sunset closes the route.

The trick is not the restaurants, it's the sequence. Pickle, steam-table, coffee, lahmacun, ice, rakı. Every other order breaks the afternoon.

Distances are walking. Total route ~1.6 km. Vegetarian: doable at every stop. Vegan: stops 1, 2, 3, 5 only.
