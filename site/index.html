The house rubric

We rate food, not marble.

Every restaurant we cover gets scored out of 10 across five axes: cooking, hospitality, setting, value, and the "would you come back Tuesday?" test. We weight cooking highest. We don't bump scores because the chef knows us. We eat anonymously and pay our own bill — and we say so on every review.

The most useful review is one that tells you when to go, what to order, and honestly — whether to bother.
