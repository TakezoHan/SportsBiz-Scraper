Karaköy Lokantası: the modern classic that still knows exactly what it is.

Turquoise tiles, white tablecloths, waiters who've been here longer than some of the reviewers writing about them. Lunch is the move.

8.8/10 KaraköyModern TurkishLunch₺₺₺

By Deniz Aksoy · Updated 8 April 2026 · Visited anonymously, twice for lunch, once for dinner. Bill paid in full.

Karaköy Lokantası is the argument against cynicism about hotel-district dining in Istanbul. It opened in 2008, it charges a grown-up price, it sits a block from a cruise terminal, and yet every time I walk in I find the same thing: the same lunch table ritual, the same deeply good mezzes, and a kitchen that has never once coasted on its postcard.

What to order

  • Topik — Armenian chickpea-and-tahini ball with cumin and currants. Perfect.
  • Lakerda — salt-cured palamut, when it's in season. Ask before ordering.
  • Manti — the lunchtime portion is half the price of dinner and twice as satisfying.
  • Künefe — the one dish you are obliged to share.
At lunch, this is one of the best-value white-tablecloth rooms in the city. At dinner, it's a different restaurant wearing the same clothes — more expensive, fussier, still excellent.

When to go

Lunch, always. The room is brightest at 13:00 when the sun comes through the water side of the street and the tile glows. Dinner is more formal and a lot pricier; skip it on a first visit.

How we scored it

Cooking 8.9 · Hospitality 9.2 · Setting 9.0 · Value 8.0 · Come-back-Tuesday 8.6 = 8.8. The value line is where the score lives or dies — lunch gets a 9, dinner a 7, and the weighted result splits the difference.
