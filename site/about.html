About

We eat anonymously. We pay our bill. We say so on every review.

Sofra Notes is an independent Istanbul restaurant publication. No paid placements. No sponsored content inside reviews. When something is ever paid, it'll be labelled in 24-point red type at the top.

The rubric

Every restaurant gets scored 1–10 across five axes, then weighted:

  • Cooking (40%) — technique, sourcing, seasoning, whether the dish is the best version of itself we've eaten this year.
  • Hospitality (20%) — pacing, knowledge, warmth. Discounted if we feel "handled" for being foreign, press, or regulars.
  • Setting (15%) — room, light, noise, smell, comfort.
  • Value (15%) — what you get for the bill, scored against that price bracket only (a ₺300 lunch and a ₺3,000 dinner are judged separately).
  • Come-back-Tuesday (10%) — would we eat here on a random weekday by ourselves? This is the honesty check.

Every review shows the individual axis scores and the weighted total.

Ethics & disclosures

  • We visit anonymously, under our own names, without booking as press.
  • We pay the full bill, including drinks, on every visit. Receipts are archived.
  • We do not accept comped meals. Ever.
  • We do not sell advertising to restaurants we cover.
  • We visit at least twice before publishing; three times for anything scored above 9.0.
  • We re-check every review twice a year and publish the last re-check date.
  • We correct errors on the page, visibly, with a dated note.
