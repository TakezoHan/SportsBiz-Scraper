25 Hidden Gems

Last updated 14 Apr 2026

The places we'd send our best friend to before they ever hit a guidebook. Criteria: ≤ 40 seats, mostly locals, no Michelin listing, and a dish we think about when we're not there.

  1. Asmalı Cavit · Beyoğlu

    Genuine meyhane. No stage, no tourist menu. The rakı list goes nine pages; the fish is bought that morning.

    9.0/10

  2. Karaköy Gümrük · Karaköy

    A single room, a blackboard menu, and some of the best grilled lüfer in Istanbul when the season comes.

    8.7/10

  3. Kiva Han · Nişantaşı

    Southeastern home cooking hidden behind a dry-cleaner's. The mumbar is the benchmark.

    8.6/10

  4. Ali Ocakbaşı · Kuzguncuk

    Five tables, a wood fire, a grandfather who doesn't like when you skip the liver.

    8.5/10

  5. Zübeyir Ocakbaşı · Beyoğlu

    Not actually "hidden" anymore, but still the cleanest, smokiest kebap room on the European side.

    8.4/10
